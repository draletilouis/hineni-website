Album photos

Everything in the album, plus the photos on the map. Tap a photo to replace it: the new one shows everywhere the old one did. Your changes are kept as a draft: preview them, then publish to put them on the website.

Drop photos here or JPEG, PNG or WebP, the full-size originals (up to 40 MB each). Location data is removed.

    Spots on the page

    Each of these places on the website shows one chosen photo. Swap any of them for another photo from the album, or upload a new one. Each photo works best in one spot only.

    Clips

    Every video on the website: the wildlife sightings, the experience clips and the film. Tap a clip to replace it everywhere it plays, or upload a new one here and choose where it plays in Spots on the page.

    Drop clips here or MP4 (H.264) or WebM, up to 250 MB each. Short clips (under a minute) load fastest.
