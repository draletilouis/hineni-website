# Running the Hineni website

Everything on the website can be changed from the admin portal: photos, clips, wording, prices, contact
details, trips and who can sign in. You don't need a developer or any code.

**Where:** your website's address followed by `/admin`, for example `hinenisafaris.com/admin`.
Sign in with your email and password. It works on a computer or a phone.

## How changes work: draft, preview, publish

Nothing you do in the portal goes on the website straight away. Every change is saved to a **draft**, and
the bar at the top of the portal tells you when there are unpublished changes.

1. **Make your changes** in any tab. Each one saves when you leave the box, or straight away for buttons and
   switches.
2. **Preview the website.** This opens the website with your draft, exactly as visitors will see it. A green
   bar across the top reminds you it's a preview. Only signed-in admins can see it.
3. **Publish.** Everyone visiting the website now sees your changes.

Changed your mind? **Discard changes** throws the draft away and puts everything back to the live website.

## Photos

**Album photos** holds every photo on the website, grouped by trip.

- **Replace a photo:** tap it and choose the new one from your phone or computer. The new photo shows everywhere
  the old one did (the album, the map and any spot on the page) and keeps its caption, trip and type. The old
  one goes under **Removed**: **Put back** there undoes the swap.
- **Add photos:** drag them onto the box at the top, or choose them from your computer or phone. Pick the
  trip and what they show first. Use the originals (JPEG, PNG or WebP, at least 1000 pixels wide), not
  thumbnails or screenshots. Locations hidden in the photos are removed automatically.
- **Captions:** click under a photo and type what's happening and where. A gold box means it has no caption
  yet. Captions help visitors using screen readers, and Google.
- **Move or sort:** change the trip or "what it shows" under each photo.
- **Put on the page:** under any photo, choose where it should show: a spot on the page (it takes that
  spot's place) or a place or stopover on the map (it's added to its photos).
- **Remove from website:** hides a photo. Find it under **Removed** to put it back, or delete it for good.

**Spots on the page** shows every place on the website with one chosen photo or clip: the big slideshow at the
top (on computers and on phones), trip cards and each day of the itineraries, experiences, the wildlife
sightings, the Ugly 5, the photos set into "The idea", the big quote, guest stories, the group portrait and the film.

- Click a photo to **choose another one** from the album, or upload a new one there and then.
- Spots marked for landscape or portrait photos show those first; others will be cropped.
- **Back to the original** undoes your change to that spot.

## Clips

**Clips** lists every video on the website. To swap one, tap it and choose the new clip: it plays everywhere the
old one did (**Put back** on the old one undoes it). To add one, drag it onto the box: any clip from a phone or camera (MP4, MOV and the rest), up to 250 MB.

The website converts every clip so it plays on every phone and browser, with a lighter copy for phones. Give it
a minute or two after the upload finishes; a long clip takes longer. The opening image is taken from the clip.
Then choose where it plays: **Put on the page** under the clip, or **Spots on the page** (experience clips,
wildlife sightings, the film) or the **Map**. A clip can only be deleted when nothing is using it.

If a clip is refused with "couldn't be read", export it again from your phone or editing app.

## Map

The **Map** tab lists every stop and place on the route map, and the stopovers on each trip's drive.

- **Photos and clips:** each one has a row of its photos and clips. **+ Add photos or clips** opens the
  album: tap to add, tap again to take it off; you can upload new ones there too. The first is the cover;
  **←** moves one earlier and **✕** takes it off. Clips play when a visitor taps them.
- **Add a stopover:** under each trip, give it a name, a time, a note, and where it is: in Google Maps, press
  and hold on the spot and copy the coordinates (or share the place and paste its link). It pops up, with its
  photos, as that trip's drive passes it on the map. Change or delete it any time. Once it's added, the album
  opens so you can give it its photos and clips straight away.
- **The places the website came with** (including the national parks: Bwindi, Mgahinga, Lake Mburo, Semuliki,
  Mount Elgon and Kidepo): change a place's **name**, the line **about it**, whether it's **a paid extra**, and
  **Show on the map** (turned off, Explore, Build your own and the drives leave it out). Its position stays as
  built. **Back to the original** undoes all of it, photos included.
- **Add a place:** under each region (Fort Portal, Queen Elizabeth, Murchison, Moyo, the national parks, and
  "On the road from Kampala"), give the new place a name, where it is (as for a stopover), one line about it, and whether it's a
  paid extra. The map looks up the roads to it from the places around it, so visitors can tour it with the
  region in **Explore** and pick it in **Build your own**, and the drive goes there on the real roads. Then add
  its photos and clips. If the road lookup fails (no internet at that moment) the map draws a straight line and
  says so in the portal: change its position, or paste it again, to try again.
- **Regions:** each region (destination) has its own box at the top of its section: its name, a colour for its
  roads on the map, the place its tour starts from, a line about it for **Explore**, and **Show in Explore**.
  The box also has **Best time to go**, **What you'll see** and **Good to know** (age limits, start times, what
  to bring): they show on the region's Explore card under **Before you go**. Empty one to leave it out.
  On the website, every region's card in **Explore** also has **Drive from Kampala**: the whole drive there on
  the real roads, with the kilometres and the towns on the way, then around the region.
- **Stopovers on the drive from Kampala:** under each region, below its places. Add one the same way as a trip's
  stopover (a name, a time, where it is, a note), then give it photos and clips. It pops up, with its photos, as
  **Drive from Kampala** passes it, and on the region's own tour when it's on that road. It must be on (or near)
  the road the map drives: one more than about 10 km off it won't show. Change or delete it any time.
  Changes to the regions the site came with can be undone with **Back to the original**.
- **Add a region:** at the bottom of the Map tab. Give the region a name, a line about it and a colour, and its
  first place: a name and where it is (a Google Maps link or coordinates, as above). The map finds the roads to
  it, and the region appears in **Explore** and **Build your own**. Then add its photos and more places to it.
  **Delete this region** removes it and the places added to it (their photos stay in the album).

## Words & details

All the wording on the website, in sections you open one at a time:

- **Contact details:** email, WhatsApp number (country code and number, digits only), phone, and where you are.
  They're set to info@hinenisafaris.com and +256 702 116926.
- **Front page:** the line under the photos at the top.
- **Each trip:** name, **group size** (for example "2 to 12 people"; empty hides it), **prices by group size**
  (one row per group size: how many travel, and the price per person, for example 2 → USD 650, 3–4 → USD 520,
  5+ → USD 450; **+ Add a price** adds a row, **Remove** takes one off), a **headline price** (leave it empty to
  lead with the lowest price on the list, or with no prices at all, "Quote within 24 hours"), length, best season,
  route and description. The trip cards, the day-by-day plan and the map show them.
- **Guest stories:** each guest's name, trip, the worry they had and the moment it went away. The three stories
  there now are samples: replace their words with real guests', then turn on **show on the website**.
- **Questions:** each question and answer, with a switch to hide one.
- **Experiences, Wildlife and Who we are:** titles and descriptions.
- **The Ugly 5 and Did you know?:** each animal's name and note, and each fact. The Ugly 5 photos are in
  **Spots on the page**: the warthog and the marabou stork have none yet, so add one when you have it.

A gold **Changed** label shows what you've edited; **Back to the original** undoes it.

## Trips

Rename a trip, change the month it ran, or add a new trip. Each trip is a chapter of the photo album. A new
trip can be deleted until it has photos.

## Team

- **Add someone:** their email and a password of at least 8 characters. Send them the password separately;
  they can change it once they've signed in.
- **Remove** someone and they're signed out straight away.
- **Change your password:** you'll need your current one.

Team changes apply immediately; they aren't part of the draft.

## History: going back

Every time you publish, the version it replaced is kept, back to **the website as built**. To go back:

1. Open **History** and choose **Restore into the draft** on the version you want.
2. **Preview** it.
3. **Publish** it.

Restoring never loses photos or clips you've uploaded since: their photos are kept under **Removed** in the
album, and their clips stay in Clips.

## If something goes wrong

- **"You were signed out":** sign in again. Sessions last 12 hours.
- **A photo is refused:** it's probably too small (use the original) or an iPhone HEIC file from a computer.
  Upload from the iPhone's browser instead, which converts it, or export it as JPEG.
- **Can't remove a photo:** it's chosen for a spot on the page. Choose a different photo for that spot first.
- **The website looks wrong after publishing:** restore the previous version from History and publish it.
