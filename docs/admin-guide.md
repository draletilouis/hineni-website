# Running the Hineni website

Everything on the website can be changed from the admin portal: photos, clips, wording, prices, contact
details, trips and who can sign in. You don't need a developer or any code.

**Where:** your website's address followed by `/admin`, for example `hinenisafaris.com/admin`.
Sign in with your email and password. It works on a computer or a phone.

## How changes work: draft, preview, publish

Nothing you do in the portal goes on the website straight away. Every change is saved to a **draft**, and
the bar at the top of the portal tells you when there are unpublished changes.

1. **Make your changes** in any tab. Each one saves when you leave the box, or straight away for buttons and
   switches.
2. **Preview the website.** This opens the website with your draft, exactly as visitors will see it. A green
   bar across the top reminds you it's a preview. Only signed-in admins can see it.
3. **Publish.** Everyone visiting the website now sees your changes.

Changed your mind? **Discard changes** throws the draft away and puts everything back to the live website.

## Photos

**Album photos** holds every photo on the website, grouped by trip.

- **Replace a photo:** tap it and choose the new one from your phone or computer. The new photo shows everywhere
  the old one did (the album, the map and any spot on the page) and keeps its caption, trip and type. The old
  one goes under **Removed**: **Put back** there undoes the swap.
- **Add photos:** drag them onto the box at the top, or choose them from your computer or phone. Pick the
  trip and what they show first. Use the originals (JPEG, PNG or WebP, at least 1000 pixels wide), not
  thumbnails or screenshots. Locations hidden in the photos are removed automatically.
- **Captions:** click under a photo and type what's happening and where. A gold box means it has no caption
  yet. Captions help visitors using screen readers, and Google.
- **Move or sort:** change the trip or "what it shows" under each photo.
- **Remove from website:** hides a photo. Find it under **Removed** to put it back, or delete it for good.

**Spots on the page** shows every place on the website with one chosen photo or clip: the big slideshow at the
top (on computers and on phones), trip cards and each day of the itineraries, experiences, the wildlife
sightings, the photos set into "The idea", the big quote, guest stories, the group portrait and the film.

- Click a photo to **choose another one** from the album, or upload a new one there and then.
- Spots marked for landscape or portrait photos show those first; others will be cropped.
- **Back to the original** undoes your change to that spot.

## Clips

**Clips** lists every video on the website. To swap one, tap it and choose the new clip: it plays everywhere the
old one did (**Put back** on the old one undoes it). To add one, drag it onto the box. Use **MP4** (most phones and
editing apps export this) or WebM, up to 250 MB. Short clips, under a minute, load fastest for visitors.

The portal takes the opening image from the clip itself. Once a clip is uploaded, choose where it plays in
**Spots on the page** (experience clips, wildlife sightings or the film). A clip can only be deleted when no
spot is using it.

If a clip is refused with "won't play in a browser", export it again as MP4 (H.264).

## Words & details

All the wording on the website, in sections you open one at a time:

- **Contact details:** email, WhatsApp number (country code and number, digits only), phone, and where you are.
  They're set to info@hinenisafaris.com and +256 702 116926.
- **Front page:** the line under the photos at the top.
- **Each trip:** name, length, best season, route, description and **price** (leave the price empty to show "Quote
  within 24 hours").
- **Guest stories:** each guest's name, trip, the worry they had and the moment it went away. The three stories
  there now are samples: replace their words with real guests', then turn on **show on the website**.
- **Questions:** each question and answer, with a switch to hide one.
- **Experiences, Wildlife and Who we are:** titles and descriptions.

A gold **Changed** label shows what you've edited; **Back to the original** undoes it.

## Trips

Rename a trip, change the month it ran, or add a new trip. Each trip is a chapter of the photo album. A new
trip can be deleted until it has photos.

## Team

- **Add someone:** their email and a password of at least 8 characters. Send them the password separately;
  they can change it once they've signed in.
- **Remove** someone and they're signed out straight away.
- **Change your password:** you'll need your current one.

Team changes apply immediately; they aren't part of the draft.

## History: going back

Every time you publish, the version it replaced is kept, back to **the website as built**. To go back:

1. Open **History** and choose **Restore into the draft** on the version you want.
2. **Preview** it.
3. **Publish** it.

Restoring never loses photos or clips you've uploaded since: their photos are kept under **Removed** in the
album, and their clips stay in Clips.

## If something goes wrong

- **"You were signed out":** sign in again. Sessions last 12 hours.
- **A photo is refused:** it's probably too small (use the original) or an iPhone HEIC file from a computer.
  Upload from the iPhone's browser instead, which converts it, or export it as JPEG.
- **Can't remove a photo:** it's chosen for a spot on the page. Choose a different photo for that spot first.
- **The website looks wrong after publishing:** restore the previous version from History and publish it.
