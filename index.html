The idea

Uganda is the Africa people forget to imagine. Tuskers wading in the Nile. Lions on the rift floor. Volcanic lakes stacked between green ridges. The world's longest river forced through a gap seven metres wide. We take small groups there — close, loud, and unfiltered.
